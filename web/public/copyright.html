Copyright & Takedown

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Aperture does not host content. It only fetches a public post when a visitor pastes its link, and the file is deleted from our server within about 30 minutes. There is no library, gallery or search of downloaded media. The original stays on the platform where it was posted.

We respect creators

Aperture is meant for saving content people have the right to keep. Our Terms of Use forbid using it to infringe copyright or to re-upload other people's work.

The most effective step

Because the content lives on the original platform, the fastest way to remove it everywhere is to report it to that platform: Instagram, Facebook, X or Pinterest. Once a post is removed or made private there, Aperture can no longer fetch it.

Sending us a notice

If you are a rights holder (or authorised to act for one) and believe Aperture is being used to infringe your rights, you can ask us to block specific links. Send a notice through our takedown form. You can use GitHub's form or your own wording, but please include:

  1. The work you own, or a link to it.
  2. The exact post URL(s) you want blocked on Aperture.
  3. Your relationship to the work (owner, or authorised agent).
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate.
  6. How we can reach you if we need more information.

Takedown requests on GitHub are public. Don't put your home address, phone number or other private details in the form. A name or organisation and a business contact are enough.

What happens next

  • We aim to acknowledge notices within a few days.
  • For a valid notice, we'll block the reported links on Aperture so they can no longer be fetched through it.
  • We may ask for more information if a notice is incomplete or unclear.
  • Repeated abuse can lead us to block links, platforms or visitors.

Grievances

Other complaints about the Service, including under India's Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, can be raised through the same contact form. Please don't include personal details in public issues.

Trademarks

Aperture is not affiliated with Meta Platforms, X Corp or Pinterest. Platform names are used only to describe which links the Service accepts.

False notices

Please only send a notice if you own the rights or are authorised to act for the owner. Knowingly false claims may carry legal consequences.
