Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies and no database. To work, the server briefly handles the link you paste and the file you download, and our hosting provider keeps routine technical logs. Your history stays in your own browser.

This policy covers the Aperture website at aperture.advaitachandra.in, run by the Aperture operator (“we”, “us”).

What we handle, and why

Links you paste

The server needs the link to fetch the post. Links are held in memory while your request is processed and then discarded. When something fails, the error message written to the server log can include the link.

Downloaded files

The media file is written to the server's temporary storage so you can download it, and is deleted automatically within about 30 minutes. We don't look at, keep or share these files.

Your IP address

Like every website, the server sees your IP address. Aperture uses it only in memory, to apply fair-use limits (requests per minute and downloads at once). It is not written to a database or to Aperture's own logs. Our hosting and network providers may record it in standard access logs (see below).

Things stored on your device

Your settings and download history are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and you can turn history off or clear it in Settings. The installable app also keeps an offline copy of the page in your browser's cache. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts, sign-ups or email lists.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or fingerprinting.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • Fonts and icons are served from our own server, so your browser doesn't contact third-party font or script services.

Services that see some data

  • Cloudflare (privacy policy) sits in front of the site for DNS, security and delivery. Your requests, including your IP address, pass through it.
  • Render (our hosting provider, privacy policy) runs the server and keeps technical and access logs, which can include IP addresses, times and requested pages, for a limited period.
  • The platform you link to (Instagram, X, Facebook or Pinterest) receives a request from our server, not from you, for the post you asked for.
  • Apify (privacy policy) receives the link to an Instagram post only when the normal method fails and the backup is used. No information about you is sent to it.

How long data is kept

  • Links and job details: in memory, cleared within about 30 minutes.
  • Downloaded files: deleted within about 30 minutes.
  • Server logs: kept by the hosting provider under its own retention period.
  • Browser storage: until you clear it.

Children

The Service doesn't ask for names, emails or ages, and doesn't knowingly collect personal data from anyone. If you're under 18, please use it with a parent's or guardian's knowledge.

Your choices and rights

You can clear your history and settings at any time from your browser or the app's Settings. Because we don't keep accounts or a database, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you have a privacy question or a request under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, contact us using the link below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.

Contact

Use the contact form on GitHub. GitHub issues are public, so don't include personal information. Describe your request and we'll reply there.
